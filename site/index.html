    Detailed readings and data labels

    The last 24 hours with every label. Snow depth is shown as measured here, including small negative values: zero is the top of the alpine heather under the sensor, and the sensor scatters by about ±8 cm. Provisional values have only had automatic checks; Approved ones have been reviewed; values are provisional unless a line under them says otherwise.
