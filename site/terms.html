1. What this is

POW-O-METER is an automatic snow and weather station near the top of Shames Mountain, BC. The Mount Remo Backcountry Society (MRBS) owns it, and volunteers run it. This page also shows readings from nearby stations and images from Shames Mountain's webcams, used with their owners' permission. Nobody watches any of this around the clock.

2. Not a forecast

The readings describe one spot at one time. They are not an avalanche forecast, a weather forecast, or an assessment of whether terrain is safe. Snow depth at a station can differ greatly from nearby slopes, because of wind, aspect and elevation. Always check the avalanche forecast at avalanche.ca, and make your own decisions in the field.

3. Readings can be wrong, late or missing

Stations are unattended instruments in harsh conditions. Readings may be wrong (for example, because of falling snow, rime or a damaged sensor), late (satellite or processing delays) or missing altogether. The page shows how old each value is: an old value is not current conditions. “Provisional” values have only had automatic checks. Volunteers may correct them later, and “Approved” values have been reviewed.
