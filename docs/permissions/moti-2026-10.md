# Permission: BC Ministry of Transportation & Infrastructure (MoTI)

**Owner:** BC Ministry of Transportation & Infrastructure, weather network
program.

**Data:** observations from the Ministry's stations near Shames Mountain, as
served by Avalanche Canada's station API:

| Station id | Name |
|---|---|
| `avcan_56` | Kasiks High |
| `avcan_57` | Kasiks Low |
| `avcan_58` | Shames (bottom of the hill) |

**Granted:** in writing, received 2026-10-05 by the maintainer for the Mount
Remo Backcountry Society.

**This file is a record, not the Ministry's text.** The Ministry asked that
its reply not be given to AI tools. The maintainer wrote this summary of its
terms, and keeps the original reply outside this repository. Do not ask for
it, paste it into an AI assistant or add it here.

**What it covers** (checked against the original by the maintainer,
2026-10-16):
- showing the data on the station page, including charts;
- a permanent public copy of the readings as received, in `raw/avcan/` in
  this repository;
- the published files people can download (`nearby_best.csv`,
  `nearby_recent.csv`, `nearby_latest.json`).
- the same use in powWX, the maintainer's separate forecast project.

**Conditions:**
1. The Ministry is credited for the data.
2. It is stated that the Ministry is not liable.

**How POW-O-METER meets them:** the `licence` text of each station in
`config/stations.yaml` gives both. It is published with every station in
`sites.json` and shown under the charts on the station page. The terms of use
(`site/terms.html`, sections 5 and 6) and `LICENSE-DATA.md` repeat them.

**If the Ministry withdraws permission:** set `enabled` and `publish` to
false for the three stations in `config/stations.yaml`. The page and the
published files then stop showing the data at the next build. The copy in
`raw/avcan/` stays in the repository and its history. If the Ministry asks
for that copy to be removed too, that would be an exception to CLAUDE.md
rule 1 (raw files are never deleted), for MRBS to decide deliberately.
